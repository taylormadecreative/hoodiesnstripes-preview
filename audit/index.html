Website review · hoodiesnstripes.com

seven things costing you sales

And the fix for each one. Checked on the live store on October 7, 2026, on phone and desktop, with every finding confirmed two ways. Ranked by how much each one costs you.

Already working

  • The old hoodiesnslides.com address forwards to the new store.
  • Express checkout (Shop Pay and others) is on the product pages.
  • A deep catalog: 47 distinct styles across 17 schools.

The new design keeps all of this.

What it costs and the fix

01

There’s no way for a fan to give you their email.

Every visitor who isn’t ready to buy today leaves with no way back. There’s no signup in the header, the footer, the cart or the collection pages, so a new drop only reaches the people who happen to catch the Instagram post.

The fix A signup in the footer of every page, a one-time popup, “notify me” on sold-out sizes and the checkout opt-in. Every signup is tagged with the school the fan picks, so a FAMU drop goes to FAMU fans.

02

The site never links to your Instagram.

Instagram is where the energy is, with reels pulling tens of thousands of views. A shopper on the site has no way to get there, and never sees that proof.

The fix Instagram in the header and footer, and a reels section on the homepage that plays your latest posts.

03

The same hoodie is listed several times.

26 items are listed more than once, 53 extra listings in all. The Colorado Buffaloes Letterman Hoodie alone appears 5 times, once for each color. Shoppers scroll past what looks like the same item, and the copies compete with each other in Google.

The fix One product per hoodie, with every color as an option on it.

04

Most products have a single photo.

66 of 100 listings have exactly one photo, and 1 has none. Hoodies sell on the details: the chenille, the patches, the stripes, how it fits.

The fix Front, back, detail and on-body photos for every product, like the Vanderbilt shoot that leads the new homepage.

05

The brand shows up under two names.

The store says Hoodies+Stripes, but the gift card is still the “Hoodies + Slides Gift Card”, the contact email in the footer is info@hoodiesnslides.com, and the Instagram highlights still carry the Hoodies+Slides pennant. It makes a shopper wonder if they’re in the right place.

The fix One name everywhere: the gift card, the contact email, the highlights and the captions.

06

Products are filed in the wrong categories.

19 products have the wrong product type or none at all. Joggers, socks and slides are filed as “Fleece Hoodies”, so filters, Google Shopping and any future automation treat them as hoodies.

The fix The right product type on every item, so every collection and filter builds itself.

07

Google sees a store name, not what you sell.

The homepage title is just “Hoodies+Stripes”, with nothing about letterman hoodies or the schools people search for. The image that shows when the link is shared points to an insecure http:// address.

The fix Titles and descriptions written for the searches that matter, and a share card made for the brand.

How the switch works

  1. Built on your Shopify store. The new design goes in as a new theme. Your products, orders, customers and checkout stay exactly where they are.
  2. Your current theme stays saved. It sits in your theme library, so switching back is one click.
  3. The catalog gets cleaned up first. Duplicate listings become one product with color options, and every item gets the right product type.
  4. Email starts working on day one. Signups flow into your Shopify customer list, tagged by school, ready for the first drop email.
